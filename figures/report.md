## What was run

- Train pool, cut-off 2021-12-31: 56,572 buildings, 4,847 adopters (first PV 2022-01-01 to 2023-12-31), base rate 8.6%.
- Test pool, cut-off 2023-12-31: 51,725 buildings, 4,020 adopters (first PV 2024-01-01 to 2025-12-31), base rate 7.8%.
- Robustness label `y_2024`: same test pool and ranking, first PV in 2024: 2,668 adopters, base rate 5.2%.
- Models:
  - `random` (Random): reference: a random ranking (expected values, no fitting).
  - `roof_yield` (Roof yield): baseline 1: rank by the yearly electricity yield of the suitable roof.
  - `nbr_share` (Neighbour PV share): baseline 2: rank by the share of buildings within 250 m that already have PV.
  - `lasso` (LASSO): L1-penalised logistic regression, class weights, penalty chosen by cross-validation grouped by municipality (the headline model).
  - `mlp` (Neural net (16)): small neural net (scikit-learn MLP, one hidden layer of 16) on the LASSO features.
  - `mlp_wide` (Neural net (32, 16)): small neural net (hidden layers 32 and 16) on the LASSO features.
  - `lasso_heat_source` (LASSO + heating source): sensitivity run: LASSO plus the heating source (leakage-contaminated).
  - `lasso_heat_date` (LASSO + heating source and date): sensitivity run: LASSO plus heating source and record date (leakage-contaminated).
- Intervals: 95% percentile intervals from a cluster bootstrap that resamples whole municipalities, B = 200, seed 0; the same draws serve every model, so gaps between models are paired.

## How to read the metrics

- Capture at k: share of all adopters found in the top k of the ranking. Random gives k.
- ROC AUC: probability that a random adopter is ranked above a random non-adopter. Random gives 0.5.
- Average precision: area under the precision-recall curve. Random gives the base rate.
- Hit rate (top 10%): share of adopters among the buildings ranked in the top 10%. Lift: hit rate divided by the base rate; random gives 1.

## Limits

- Register lag: 2025 is not fully registered, so the 2024-2025 label is understated for the last months; `y_2024` is the check.
- Roof coverage: the roof file is the 2023 release; buildings without a roof record are dropped from the pool and adopt less.
- Snapshot register: attributes are today's values; demolished buildings are missing.
- Same buildings in train and test: out-of-time test, not out-of-sample across buildings.
- Tariff and heating effects are not identified.
- No causal claims.
